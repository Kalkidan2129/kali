
# Revenue Forecasting and Performance Analysis of Vodafone Qatar (2021–2023)

## Project Overview

This project transformed Vodafone Qatar's telecom data into a unified, machine learning-ready dataset to forecast revenue and analyze performance from 2021 to 2023. Using NexusMax, Python, and Microsoft Fabric, it uncovered patterns in revenue, churn, and customer behavior, supporting smarter financial and strategic decision-making. The final solution includes a comprehensive Power BI dashboard visualizing key KPIs for actionable insights.

---

## Business Problem

Telecom revenue performance varies widely across regions and customer segments, but the business lacked a consolidated view to identify growth areas and revenue risks. Fluctuations in churn and subscriber behavior created forecasting uncertainty, hindering confident budget planning. Without integrated visibility into revenue, churn, and network performance, finance and strategy teams struggled to make data-driven decisions.

---

## Objective

- Create a unified, clean dataset combining revenue, churn, and customer metrics
- Improve quarterly revenue forecasting accuracy to optimize budget allocation
- Reduce churn in key telecom segments to protect recurring revenue

---

---

## Project Workflow

- Data extraction and cleaning from Vodafone Qatar dataset
- Feature engineering and creation of ML-ready dataset
- Exploratory data analysis to identify revenue and churn patterns
- Supervised regression modeling for quarterly revenue forecasting
- Development of Power BI dashboards for KPI visualization and insights

---

## Key Insights

- Total revenue increased 14% quarter-over-quarter, driven mainly by Doha region
- Churn rate for high-value prepaid customers rose by 0.3% QoQ, impacting revenue stability
- Network availability remained high at 99.95%, supporting customer retention efforts
- Churn in Mobile Postpaid and Fixed Broadband segments exceeded overall churn, risking $8–12K annual revenue loss
